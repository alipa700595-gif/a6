Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Pupil Read Stone Academy

1. Commitment to Student and Family Data Privacy

Pupil Read Stone Academy ("we," "our," or "the Academy") operates pupilreadstone.com. We are dedicated to maintaining the absolute confidentiality, security, and privacy of student diagnostic records, parent contact data, and visitor information in full compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and FERPA-aligned educational privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal necessary personal and academic data strictly for educational admissions, diagnostic scheduling, and digital journal delivery:

  • Direct Admissions Data: Student name, parent/guardian contact information, telephone number, grade level, and diagnostic assessment notes submitted via our inquiry forms.
  • Aggregated Technical Data: Anonymized browsing metrics, device types, referring URLs, and page visit durations collected through Google Analytics 4 (Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01).

3. Data Usage & Third-Party Protection

We do not sell, lease, or distribute student or family data to third-party commercial marketers. Information is shared strictly with secure technical infrastructure providers (such as encrypted cloud hosting and SSL/TLS transmission channels) required for academic operations.

4. Your Legal Rights

Parents, guardians, and adult scholars retain the right to inspect personal educational records, request corrections to inaccurate data, or request permanent deletion from our databases at any time.
