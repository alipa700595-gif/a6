Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Pupil Read Stone Academy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store user preferences, maintain session state, and compile aggregated site usage statistics.

2. Types of Cookies We Utilize

  • Essential Technical Cookies: Strictly necessary for core navigation, mobile menu rendering, and form security.
  • Performance Analytics Cookies: Powered by Google Analytics 4 (G-0LY0HY7L01) to measure total visitor volume and popular educational articles without tracking personal student identities.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can configure your browser to accept, reject, or delete cookies at any time through your browser security settings.
